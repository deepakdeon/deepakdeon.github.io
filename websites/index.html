Palaikari Fish Farm
Booking website
An online booking website built for Palaikari, the tourism centre of Matsyafed, a Government of Kerala undertaking. The farm’s basic information, attractions, activities and food are showcased with photos. A language switch lets you read everything on the page in Malayalam or English. Ticket rates, add-on services and a booking page are included, and each can be selected and booked separately. No payment gateway is added. UPI payments are verified manually over WhatsApp.

EJ Table Tennis Tournament
Live points page
A web page built so everyone can follow the live points table of the table tennis tournament held for EJ’s Fitness 7th Anniversary. It runs on a Google Sheet. Scores updated in the sheet after each match appear on the web page every 15 seconds. In the group stage, the points difference makes it easy to see who is ahead. The person managing it only has to enter the scores in the Google Sheet.

Old Bridge Cafe
Menu website
The menu web page for Old Bridge Cafe. It carries the cafe’s details, location, Instagram page link and Google review link. Being an open-air cafe and a tourist spot, its header background changes with the local weather. A language switch right at the top lets you read the menu in English or Malayalam. The main page lists all categories with attractive photos and prices. Tap a category to open a detailed page of its dishes, and swipe to move between categories.
